# Book Outline

Title: Marvin's Guide to Sleeping Soundly on the $RUG

Subtitle: Crypto, wallets, bounties, AI agents, and other bedtime problems

## Thesis

The safest first crypto skill is not trading. It is learning to hold a wallet, read receipts, complete useful work, and keep your financial floor intact.

This book turns crypto education into a bounty-driven learning path. Each chapter teaches a concept, then offers a contribution bounty so the reader can practice by improving the guide, building tools, or helping a project they believe in.

The book and Marvin's Hermes Agent fork compound together: the book gives the curriculum; the agent gives the reader a local tutor, safety reviewer, and submission assistant.

## Part I — The Financial Floor

### Chapter 1 — Before the Wallet: Your Financial Floor

Purpose: establish financial-health-first discipline before any crypto action.

Covers:
- do not put up more than you can lose,
- emergency fund and obligation check,
- learning capital vs stress capital,
- why bounty income is a healthier beginner goal than trading gains,
- what a wallet is,
- public address vs private key vs seed phrase,
- custodial vs self-custody,
- first wallet setup checklist,
- first transaction safety habits,
- learning log.

Bounty: produce beginner-safe wallet setup improvements, glossary entries, diagrams, or localized versions.

### Chapter 2 — Bounties Before Bags

Purpose: teach how to earn through useful work before speculating.

Covers:
- what a bounty is,
- project belief vs blind reward chasing,
- choosing bounties that match skills,
- avoiding bounty scams,
- submission hygiene,
- predictable revenue stream as a practice, not a promise.

### Chapter 3 — The Agent at Your Shoulder

Purpose: set up Marvin's Hermes Agent fork as crypto tutor and safety assistant.

Covers:
- installing Hermes,
- using the `crypto-protection-education` branch,
- asking the agent to explain terms,
- reviewing bounty specs,
- receipt checks,
- private-key boundaries,
- why the agent must never ask for seed phrases.
