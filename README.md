# Marvin's Guide to Sleeping Soundly on the $RUG

A practical, receipt-first guide to crypto, wallets, bounties, AI agents, and not confusing a loud chart with a life plan.

This is a living book plus bounty program. The book teaches the reader enough crypto and AI literacy to contribute to real projects. The bounties turn learning into a predictable practice: complete useful work for projects you believe in, keep receipts, and build income through capability instead of panic.

## Start here: financial health first

Before you set up a wallet, buy a token, chase a bounty, or listen to a bodega cat with too much terminal access, check your financial floor.

Do not put up more than you can lose.

If losing the money would affect rent, food, debt, healthcare, family obligations, or your ability to sleep, it is not learning capital. It is stress in a trench coat.

The recommended goal of this guide is not "get rich trading crypto." The goal is to create a more predictable revenue stream by completing bounties for projects you believe in while learning how crypto systems actually work.

Speculate only with money you can lose. Build with time you can compound.

## What this teaches

- Wallet basics: what a wallet is, what private keys are, and why seed phrases are not screenshots.
- Bounty basics: how to find useful work, submit it cleanly, and avoid scams.
- Crypto mechanics: tokens, transactions, liquidity, market cap, AMMs, slippage, and receipts.
- AI operator basics: how to run Marvin's Hermes Agent fork as a companion tutor and safety assistant.
- Public accountability: how to record predictions, misses, and evidence without becoming a hype machine.

## Companion agent

The companion setup points to Marvin's Hermes Agent fork:

https://github.com/Marvin-The-Bodega-Cat/hermes-agent/tree/crypto-protection-education

The intended mode is not "AI tells you what to buy." That is forbidden by good sense and several species of despair.

The intended mode is:

- explain unfamiliar crypto terms,
- review bounty instructions,
- check whether a claim has receipts,
- help write safe submissions,
- help maintain a learning log,
- flag obvious wallet/security mistakes before they become expensive folklore.
